# Scientific review, evidence visibility and partial lessons

## Assemble a common evidence base

Give both author and reviewer: the original objective, current methodology and frozen judge; the blocked decision; supported findings, valid negatives, rejected alternatives, tentative explanations and unresolved limitations; the proposed experiment's controls, observables, branches, costs and stopping conditions; and exact evidence references.

Distinguish evidence retained in the project, evidence selected into the prompt, and evidence actually readable there. Selection happens before delivery: a full-context-retention flag says nothing about what the model received. Verify status windows, delivery limits and available read tools.

Decode compressed or layered structures before interpretation. A deterministic roundtrip does not prove that a text-only reviewer reconstructed the content, and a bundle can preserve exact bytes while omitting the primary evidence for the question at hand.

Maintain a project-owned lesson index covering supported, negative, partial, tentative and disputed lessons, each bound to evidence, tested scope, limitations and any superseding result. Make the index and the original objective visible to both parties, with an authorized read-only path to complete released evidence. Report omissions explicitly rather than substituting a summary.

On the DSH scientific handoff, inspect the bounded `research_status` discovery envelope and its exact metadata/catalog roots. Follow every declared catalog page needed to discover the complete inventory; a partial inventory is not an empty history. Read the referenced lesson bodies through `research_context` before using their findings, scope, uncertainty, supporting evidence or invalidation conditions. `body_complete=false` means the body has not been delivered. Use `research_evidence` for released supporting evidence and `research_status_read` for the complete retained status. Follow `next_offset_bytes` with the next default page until the source is reconstructed; preserve its full parent identity and distinguish a last page from a complete artifact.

Validate the actual next model request after native spill, pruning and transport. A small discovery envelope and exact retained file address establish discovery and recovery, while scientific interpretation requires the delivered bodies. Explicit delivery overflow or missing required context defers its dependent inference. Keep all negative, partial, tentative, disputed and superseded lessons available, and supply the reviewer with the same roots plus complete permitted decision-relevant plaintext when it has no read tools.

If evidence cannot be obtained under the current profile, record the missing material and affected claim, defer that inference and continue unaffected work. Do not invent a tool, exceed access authority, read protected inputs, or treat unavailable evidence as a negative.

For an advisory reconstruction, withhold only the author's preferred next choice during the first assessment while sharing the same evidence and lesson history. Admission review must still inspect the complete frozen proposal. Agent agreement is not empirical confirmation.

## Review the experiment and inference

Reconstruct the blocked question independently from the primary evidence. First check scope: if a proposed option changes the measurement approach, observable definition, representation family or inference contract, classify it as an outer-loop subject rather than an inner-loop experiment. Then check:

| Subject | Required scrutiny |
| --- | --- |
| Decision relevance | Which important choice can change under the proposed results, or which protocol/multistep obligation is necessary |
| Competing explanations | The strongest explanation the design fails to distinguish, with observable predictions and coexisting mechanisms |
| Measurement adequacy | Required observable, fidelity, precision and support; possible observer interference |
| Controls and identifiability | Held conditions, intervention specificity, interactions and confounding; more samples do not cure identical predictions |
| Stopping and uncertainty | Prospective checkpoints, useful-benefit threshold, observation units, sequential/multiple-comparison obligations, formal confirmation |
| Choice of alternative | The selected option against the strongest feasible challenger, including a cheaper evidence audit, continued sampling or scoped defer |
| Claims and partial lessons | Preserve valid prerequisite findings; do not let measurement failure reject the physical hypothesis or a narrow success qualify broader claims |
| Cost and authority | Complete marginal, qualification and review burden; unresolved estimates; expected value grants no execution authority |

## Require material corrections

For each material finding, retain the problem, exact evidence, affected claim or decision, and the smallest correction or discriminating experiment. Demand another control only when its absence can change validity or the decision; produce a finite correction rather than an expanding list of conceivable corner cases.

Approve only the supplied scope. Use request_changes for a supported correctable defect and defer when essential evidence or premises are unavailable. Keep the required native receipt fields and exact identities; put the complete public critique in supported reason/evidence fields or a bound permitted artifact. Verify the return path — a reviewer transcript or artifact reference is not automatically visible to the author, and private reviewer reasoning does not become author context; the returned decision and reasons are the shared record.

When addressing request_changes, require a material change that fixes the stated defect while preserving the old proposal, review and charges. If the correction cannot be evaluated, retain the limitation instead of repeatedly minting renamed proposals. Every new frozen manifest receives its own exact-subject review; changing review frequency is a separate measured policy question, not an efficiency shortcut.

## Keep partial lessons at their scope

At each legal checkpoint, separate:

- **Observation:** what actually ran, was measured, failed or stayed uncaptured.
- **Supported inference:** what the bound verifier and adequate evidence establish within the tested scope.
- **Tentative explanation:** a plausible mechanism still requiring a discriminating experiment.
- **Limitation:** an unavailable observable, unmet prerequisite, unsupported generalization or unresolved result.

Each reusable lesson records its supporting action/evidence/verifier identities, tested conditions and scope, uncertainty or status, the decisions it can inform, the circumstances that invalidate reuse, and any superseding or contradictory evidence. Keep negative and disputed lessons in the same discoverable index; never silently replace history with the latest success.

Keep operational status, evidence validity and scientific disposition separate. A failed measurement can be a valid negative about instrument or schedule qualification while leaving the physical hypothesis unidentified. Preserve the recorded verdict and explain its scope; do not relabel old evidence to simplify a report.

Retain coexisting facts together, for example: CPU routing and protocol checks passed; the required exposure screen failed; physical contention remains unidentified. Each supports a different decision and none substitutes for another.

Use existing project artifacts and supported plan/checkpoint/prerequisite associations. A prose lesson record is evidence-backed interpretation, not a new native schema, scientific authority or shared mutable database.

## Carry lessons into the next decision

Before freezing a successor, retrieve applicable lessons and prior rejections, name the actual evidence or context change, and include their permitted contents or an accessible read path in both contexts. State omitted or stale material explicitly; keep the brief compact without making evidence inaccessible.

After the result settles, compare it with the prospective branches and record which uncertainty and decision changed, which stayed open, useful partial findings, invalidated assumptions and actual marginal cost. Share the updated disposition with both parties. Capture, publication or approval does not prove that a lesson affected a later action.

Check later proposals for actual reuse. Track useful reviewer catches, unsupported objections, missed flaws, repeated unchanged failures, avoidable collection and unresolved attribution. Record human and model interventions separately. A historical replay is reasoning and visibility evidence only; a claim of prospective scientific benefit needs new independently assessed outcomes.
