# Scientific review, evidence visibility and partial lessons

## Establish a common evidence base

Give both author and reviewer the original objective, current methodology and frozen judge; the current blocked decision; supported findings, valid negatives, rejected alternatives, tentative explanations and unresolved limitations; the proposed experiment's controls, observables, outcome branches, costs and stopping conditions; and exact evidence references.

Make the available lesson/evidence index visible so either party can discover relevant material beyond the current packet. Distinguish evidence retained in the project, evidence selected into a prompt, and evidence actually readable in that prompt. Verify context selection, status windows, delivery limits and available read capabilities. A persistent Session or a full-byte-retention receipt does not establish that earlier findings reached the model.

Use an available authorized read-only route or preload complete released evidence when requested. Preserve source/hash/size and scope. Decode compressed structures before scientific interpretation; a deterministic roundtrip alone does not establish that a text-only model reconstructed the content. Provide a readable orientation with the original evidence accessible, rather than relying on an opaque name or a favorable summary.

On the DSH scientific handoff, inspect the bounded `research_status` discovery envelope and its exact metadata/catalog roots. Follow every declared catalog page needed to discover the complete inventory; a partial inventory is not an empty history. Read the referenced lesson bodies through `research_context` before using their findings, scope, uncertainty, supporting evidence or invalidation conditions. `body_complete=false` means the body has not been delivered. Use `research_evidence` for released supporting evidence and `research_status_read` for the complete retained status. Follow `next_offset_bytes` with the next default page until the source is reconstructed; preserve its full parent identity and distinguish a last page from a complete artifact.

Validate the actual next model request after native spill, pruning and transport. A small discovery envelope and exact retained file address establish discovery and recovery, while scientific interpretation requires the delivered bodies. Explicit delivery overflow or missing required context defers its dependent inference. Keep all negative, partial, tentative, disputed and superseded lessons available, and supply the reviewer with the same roots plus complete permitted decision-relevant plaintext when it has no read tools.

If evidence cannot be obtained under the current profile, record the missing material and affected claim. Defer that inference and continue unaffected admissible analysis. Do not invent a tool, exceed access authority, read protected inputs or treat unavailable evidence as a scientific negative.

For an advisory reconstruction evaluation, withhold only the author's preferred next choice during its first assessment. Keep evidence and lesson access common. Actual admission review must inspect the complete frozen proposal. Keep agent agreement separate from empirical confirmation.

## Review the experiment and inference

Reconstruct the blocked question independently from the supplied primary evidence. Check the following obligations:

| Review subject | Required scrutiny |
| --- | --- |
| Decision relevance | Identify which important choice can change under the proposed results, or the necessary protocol/multistep obligation. |
| Competing explanations | Check observable predictions, coexisting mechanisms and the strongest explanation the proposed design fails to distinguish. |
| Measurement adequacy | Check that the instrument supplies the required observable with adequate fidelity, precision and support; inspect possible observer interference. |
| Controls and identifiability | Check held conditions, intervention specificity, interactions and confounding; more samples do not cure identical predictions. |
| Stopping and uncertainty | Check prospective checkpoints, useful-benefit threshold, observation units, sequential/multiple-comparison obligations and formal confirmation rules. |
| Choice of alternative | Compare the selected experiment with the strongest feasible challenger, including a cheaper evidence audit, continued sampling or scoped defer when justified. |
| Claims and partial lessons | Preserve valid prerequisite findings; prevent measurement failure from rejecting the physical hypothesis and prevent a narrow success from qualifying broader claims. |
| Cost and authority | Check complete marginal/qualification/review burden, unresolved estimates and actual prerequisites; expected value supplies no execution authority. |

For each material finding, retain the problem, exact evidence, affected claim/decision and the smallest correction or discriminating experiment. Request a control when its absence can change validity or the decision. Bound corrections to the affected claim; do not expand review into every imaginable corner case.

Approve only the actual supplied scope. Use request_changes for a supported correctable defect, and defer when essential evidence or premises remain unavailable. Keep the required native receipt fields and exact identities. Return the complete public scientific critique through supported reason/evidence fields or a bound permitted artifact. Do not assume that a reviewer transcript or artifact reference will automatically be visible to the author; verify the actual return path.

When addressing request_changes, require a material change that fixes the stated defect. Preserve the old proposal, review and charges. If the correction cannot be evaluated under the available evidence, retain that limitation rather than repeatedly minting renamed proposals.

## Retain partial lessons without collapsing their scope

At each legal checkpoint, separate:

- **Observation:** What actually ran, was measured, failed or remained uncaptured.
- **Supported inference:** What the bound verifier and adequate evidence establish within the tested scope.
- **Tentative explanation:** A plausible mechanism still requiring a discriminating experiment.
- **Limitation:** An unavailable observable, unmet prerequisite, unsupported generalization or unresolved result.

For every reusable lesson, retain its supporting action/evidence/verifier identities, tested conditions and scope, uncertainty/status, decisions it can inform, circumstances that invalidate its reuse and any superseding or contradictory evidence. Include negative and disputed lessons in the same discoverable project index. Do not silently replace historical findings with the latest success.

Keep operational status, evidence validity and scientific disposition separate. A failed measurement can produce a valid negative about instrument/schedule qualification while leaving the target scientific hypothesis unidentified. Preserve the original recorded verdict and explain the scope; do not relabel old evidence to simplify a report.

For example, retain all three facts when they coexist: CPU routing/protocol checks passed; the required exposure screen failed; physical contention remains unidentified. Use the CPU finding for its prerequisite decisions, the exposure failure to avoid premature dependent work, and the unresolved mechanism to define a next diagnostic. None substitutes for the others.

Use existing project artifacts and supported plan/checkpoint/prerequisite associations for these lessons. A prose lesson record is evidence-backed interpretation, not a new native schema, scientific authority or shared mutable package database.

## Carry lessons into the next decision

Before freezing a successor, retrieve applicable lessons and prior rejections, identify the actual evidence/context change, and include their complete permitted contents or an accessible read path in the author's and reviewer's contexts. State omitted or stale material explicitly. Keep a compact brief without making full evidence inaccessible.

After the result settles, compare it with the prospective outcome branches. Retain which uncertainty changed, which decision changed or remained open, useful partial findings, invalidated assumptions and actual marginal costs. Share the updated evidence-backed disposition with both parties through supported delivery. Do not count capture, publication or approval as proof that a lesson affected a later action.

Check actual later proposals and results for reuse. Track useful reviewer catches, unsupported objections, missed flaws, repeated unchanged failures, avoidable collection and unresolved attribution. Record human/model interventions separately. Assess a historical replay as reasoning/visibility evidence; require new independently assessed outcomes for a claim of prospective scientific benefit.
